# Research report

Include question and deliverable; protocol version and success criteria; baseline and evaluator validation if applicable; evidence/search scope or experiments; objective results with units and uncertainty; Reviewer PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL checks; blocking issues; limitations and counterevidence; stop reason and remaining budget; reproducible commands, datasets, seeds, environment and evaluator versions; session/run/artifact/provenance references; workspace-relative output links and continuation actions.

Report objective metrics, quality review and blockers separately. Never collapse them to an unexplained universal score. Success needs both objective criteria and no unresolved critical validity issue. Partial coverage, exhausted budget, missing inputs and failed metrics remain explicit incomplete outcomes.
